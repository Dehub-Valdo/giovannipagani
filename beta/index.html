ABOUT

Giovanni Pagani founded his studio in 1996 in Parma, with the aim of offering architectural and design solutions driven by an unconventional elegance, where boldness and eclecticism merge with a constant pursuit of functionality.

A multifaceted architect, Giovanni Pagani's professional path extends beyond architectural design and interior design to include the industrial design of exclusive furnishing objects.

1996–2006

His passion for forms and objects enabled him, even before graduating, to begin collaborating with major Made in Italy design brands. Among the many, it is worth mentioning B&B Italia, Cappellini, Rolf, Giorgetti, and Armani Casa.

1996–2025

He designs buildings, residential interiors, and retail spaces, working in the residential, hospitality, and commercial sectors, both in Italy and abroad. Some of the destinations of his projects include: Milan, Rome, Florence, Porto Cervo, London, Miami, New York, Paris, Antwerp, Barcelona, Marbella, Moscow, San Francisco, and the Bahamas.

In Italy, he has overseen several projects for the Smeraldo Holding Group. Among his international works, particular significance is given to his collaboration with the Qatar Investment Authority, which enabled him to design two shopping malls for the Harrods brand.

Giovanni Pagani has completed numerous projects for commercial spaces in both the fashion world and design, such as Harrods, Twinliving, Max Mara Group, Pinko, Bugatti, Maserati, Diane von Fürstenberg, Golden Lady, and Pagani Automobili.

2016

He founded the GIOPAGANI brand, within which he translates certain codes borrowed from fashion, making them the foundation for the creation of exclusive objects with a strong stylistic identity.

2025

He took part in the founding and creation of DEHUB. The ambition is to go beyond the traditional architecture studio to create a Creative Hub that brings multidisciplinarity into the design approach.

PHILOSOPHY

An architect and designer, Pagani has made eclecticism his defining hallmark. In his projects, different eras, languages, and even distant references coexist in harmony, never in contrast.
